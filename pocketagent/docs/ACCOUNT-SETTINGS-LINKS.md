# Provider account controls — beta7

Reviewed 2026-09-12. PocketAgent opens fixed official browser destinations; it does not read or write provider training preferences. Authenticated account pages were not tested with a real account. Where no published direct settings link was confirmed, the row opens the official account home/dashboard and describes the navigation, or explicitly opens an official guide.

- **Codex:** ChatGPT account/security and connected apps are managed in ChatGPT settings. Codex usage uses the existing official account route. The [Data Controls FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-faq) documents the personal-account training choice and a separate full-environment choice, linking directly to `https://chatgpt.com/codex/cloud/settings/general`. The [privacy portal](https://privacy.openai.com/) also supports the personal-account opt-out. [Apps in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11487775-connectors-in-chatgpt) describes disconnection through Settings → Apps.
- **Claude:** The [official privacy instructions](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/12109829-how-do-i-change-my-model-improvement-privacy-settings) link to `https://claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls` and general settings. [Connector instructions](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11176164-use-connectors-to-extend-claude-s-capabilities) describe Customize → Connectors. Usage opens the existing `/settings/usage` account route.
- **Cursor:** [Billing help](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/billing) publishes `https://cursor.com/dashboard/billing`. Other account rows use the official dashboard. [Privacy Mode instructions](https://cursor.com/help/security-and-privacy/privacy) locate the personal setting in Cursor General settings and describe team-admin enforcement. PocketAgent links that guide; it cannot claim a native change was made.
- **Antigravity:** Google account security, connections and data/privacy links open the real Google Account pages. [Antigravity plans](https://antigravity.google/docs/plans/) links Google AI credits at `https://one.google.com/ai/credits`; quotas and overage settings remain in Antigravity. [Settings instructions](https://antigravity.google/docs/settings/) are separate from Google Account privacy. These links do not enable the unavailable native Antigravity connection.

Notifications are device-local preferences. Only authoritative engine events with stable session/event IDs can generate generic alerts. Deduplication stores up to 256 event hashes, not raw identifiers or message content. Android permission/channel policy still controls delivery. At most 12 event cards are retained; foreground-service notices are left intact. Real-device notification, browser and AppLock lifecycle testing remains required.
